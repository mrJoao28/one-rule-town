Reviewed November 24, 2026. Target: 100 total visits by Saturday, November 28, 2026. The Creator Dashboard showed 2 visits at review time, so the gap was 98. Visits cannot be guaranteed by code changes alone.

| Experience | Observable hook | Useful lesson for One Rule Town |

The current build already includes 11 server-enforced rules, six resident NPCs when the server is empty, a blue HUSS HUSS court, cosmetic progression, four developer products and two passes. Residents fill out quiet servers; they are labeled NPCs and do not count as real visits. The live listing currently restricts access to users aged 16 and older. Creator Dashboard says access for younger players needs either 100 highly engaged players or a refundable 1,000 Robux publication fee. Do not pay the fee without a spending decision.

## Launch sequence

1. Publish a verified main build, play a complete solo round on desktop and mobile, and confirm the rule warnings, Mayor movement, resident count, coins, and shop prompts. Prefer a test purchase flow only with an authorized test account.
2. Use a faithful custom thumbnail showing the actual town, a visible rule and the Mayor. The uploaded cover and icon already exist; inspect them against the new place build before replacing. Keep the title and description honest about implemented mechanics.
3. Share the direct game URL with a small group of eligible players (16+) and ask for gameplay feedback, especially first-minute confusion. Use short clips of the real court, the Mayor and a lava round; do not simulate visits or spam communities.
4. Track total visits daily through Saturday, along with first-play bounce, session time, and repeat play in Creator Analytics. If players leave early, fix their first-minute experience before buying ads or adding more monetization.

Roblox discovery documentation: https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery and https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq . A custom thumbnail is required for Home recommendations; engagement and retention matter more than raw visit count. Competitor benchmarks are a diagnostic aid, not a ranking input.
