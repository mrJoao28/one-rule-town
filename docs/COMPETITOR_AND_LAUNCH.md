# Competitor research and first 100 visits

Reviewed November 24, 2026. Target: 100 total visits by Saturday, November 28, 2026. The Creator Dashboard showed 2 visits at review time, so the gap was 98. Visits cannot be guaranteed by code changes alone.

| Experience | Observable hook | Useful lesson for One Rule Town |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [In This Town](https://www.roblox.com/games/108894291618002/In-This-Town) | A Mayor, a strong day/night split, five maps, twist cards and marked-player pursuit. | Make the Mayor recognizable and make each rule legible immediately. Keep our shorter, one-rule survival identity. |
| [In This World](https://www.roblox.com/games/123545678366291/In-This-World) | Solo/friends play and escalating rules halfway through. | Solo play must work, and a short session should show a surprising rule quickly. |
| [Disaster City](https://www.roblox.com/games/6669484102/Disaster-City) | Varied city, changing hazards, objectives. | Give the town distinct visual landmarks that players can describe and revisit. |
| [Do It Or Die](https://www.roblox.com/games/133361008835544/Do-It-Or-Die) | Seven-second random tasks and immediate elimination. | Shorten downtime while keeping our one-rule objective easy to read. |

November 24 browser comparison: the live Roblox listings showed 606.4K+ visits for In This Town and 873.0K+ for In This World. Their pages emphasize varied challenges, repeat rewards, and social play; these are examples of reach, not evidence of their revenue. In This Town listed VIP (249 Robux) and two other passes (100 and 1,000 Robux). In This World listed an early supporter pass (29 Robux) and a tip pass (3 Robux). We are keeping the existing products and server-verified receipts rather than adding an unconfigured purchase.

Live play test of One Rule Town at 1280 × 585 exposed overlapping floating signs and an offscreen bottom menu. This update reduces sign size and range, keeps signs behind geometry, anchors the bottom menu and stats inside the viewport, shows live Roblox prices in the shop, and avoids prompting for an already owned pass.

The current build includes 11 server-enforced rules, resident NPCs on quiet servers, a blue HUSS HUSS court, cosmetic progression, four developer products and two passes. This update adds an entry gate and token landmarks, NPC expressions and chatter, shorter rounds, and a first-win-of-the-day reward. Residents are labeled NPCs and do not count as real visits. The live listing currently restricts access to users aged 16 and older. Creator Dashboard says access for younger players needs either 100 highly engaged players or a refundable 1,000 Robux publication fee. Do not pay the fee without a spending decision.

## Launch sequence

1. Publish a verified main build, play a complete solo round on desktop and mobile, and confirm the rule warnings, Mayor movement, resident count, coins, and shop prompts. Prefer a test purchase flow only with an authorized test account.
2. Use a faithful custom thumbnail showing the actual town, a visible rule and the Mayor. The uploaded cover and icon already exist; inspect them against the new place build before replacing. Keep the title and description honest about implemented mechanics.
3. Share the direct game URL with a small group of eligible players (16+) and ask for gameplay feedback, especially first-minute confusion. Use short clips of the real court, the Mayor and a lava round; do not simulate visits or spam communities.
4. Track total visits daily through Saturday, along with first-play bounce, session time, and repeat play in Creator Analytics. If players leave early, fix their first-minute experience before buying ads or adding more monetization.

Roblox discovery documentation: https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery and https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq . A custom thumbnail is required for Home recommendations; engagement and retention matter more than raw visit count. Competitor benchmarks are a diagnostic aid, not a ranking input.
