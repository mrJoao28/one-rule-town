# One Rule Town — MVP Game Design

## Player promise

About every 66 seconds, the same compact town becomes a different challenge. Players should understand the current rule in under six seconds, create a funny failure worth sharing, and immediately want another round.

## Round flow

1. An 8-second intermission gathers players in the town square.
2. The town reveals one rule for six seconds.
3. All eligible players start alive at distributed spawn points.
4. The server checks the active rule every 0.1 seconds.
5. Rule breakers ragdoll and move to the spectator roof.
6. The round ends after 45 seconds, when nobody remains, or when one player remains from a multiplayer start.
7. Survivors receive rewards, including a free first win of the UTC day bonus, and the town resets for seven seconds.

## Visual direction

The MVP uses a colorful toy-town style with an unsettling civic-broadcast layer. Normal play uses cream, blue, green, and yellow. Rule danger states introduce red lights, neon surfaces, warning overlays, and the Mayor character.

The map prioritizes readable silhouettes and short travel distances over realism. Every important rule location is visible or reachable from the central square.

Sunny Square and Festival Night alternate after results. Their architecture, lighting, and outer landmarks differ, while spawn locations and tagged challenge zones remain consistent. New visitors see an original short scene of a resident on a sofa watching a fictional HUSS HUSS phone clip. The player can enter immediately or the camera returns automatically after twelve seconds.

## Progression

Players earn Town Tokens through participation, survival, and being the only survivor. Tokens buy visible effects rather than gameplay power. Wins and win streaks provide long-term goals without blocking new players.

## Monetization principles

- The free game contains the complete competitive loop.
- Permanent benefits use game passes.
- Repeatable purchases use developer products.
- Revive purchases remain as credits if they cannot be consumed immediately.
- No paid random rewards ship in the MVP.
- Product prompts never confirm or grant a purchase from a client event.

## Post-MVP roadmap

1. Rule combinations during the last 20 seconds.
2. Daily challenges and rotating cosmetic rewards.
3. A second town layout with the same rule tags.
4. Party queues and private-server rule selection.
5. Round recap cards designed for short-form video clips.
